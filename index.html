Every exterior surface, handled right

Driveway Cleaning

When cleaning driveways we blast away the surface dirt and weeds in between blocks and finish by resanding to ensure that blocks don't move with foot traffic and vehicle traffic over time, leaving a clean finished look.

Patio Cleaning

For patio cleaning surfaces such as sandstone or slabs we typically use a rotary surface cleaner to make sure we don't leave any streaks and as with driveway cleaning we finish with a chemical treatment to get rid of organic growth that pressured water alone can't remove.

Chemical Treatments (Soft Washing)

Chemical treatment is done using an X-Jet chemical sprayer leaving an even distribution of sodium hypochlorite with dwells longer than normal hand pumps because we spray it with a mix of surfactant (soap) to help the chemical stick to the surface longer before we rinse. All surrounding plants are soaked before application and then again after to dilute any possible runoff to an amount which keeps plants safe.

Fascia, Soffit & Gutter Cleaning

Cleaning of fascias, soffits and gutters using extendable poles. First comes a once over with a soap mixture to agitate and then a rinse with water to leave a clean bright finish.

Powered by professional-grade equipment running up to 4,000 PSI, matched to the right pressure for every surface.

Four stages, start to finish

01

Pressure wash

Pressure washing with either turbo nozzle or rotary surface cleaner.

02

Chemical treatment

Chemical treatment to remove what water alone can't remove by hitting the organic growth.

03

Resanding

Resanding to secure the blocks, as without they can start to shift.

04

Leaf blowing

Leaf blowing all the excess sand off meaning no excess sand is on the surface.

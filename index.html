Built on Belfast pride, not a franchise playbook

Pressure Perfect Belfast is a local, owner-operated exterior cleaning business bringing driveways, patios and homes across Greater Belfast back to their best — with clean, fast and reliable service on every job.

The business is owned and run by Max Loebnau — not a call centre, not a franchise territory, not a rotating cast of subcontractors. When you book a job, Max is the one who shows up, assesses the surface, and stands behind the result. Max was recognised as one of Belfast's "Young Entrepreneurs to Watch."

Serving the Greater Belfast area, Max treats every driveway, patio and render job with the same care, whether it's a modest terrace or a show-home patio, because reputation here is built one street at a time.
